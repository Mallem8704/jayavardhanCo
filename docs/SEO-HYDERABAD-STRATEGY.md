### 3.2 ICAI Council Guidelines Compliance Framework

The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) enforces stringent ethical guidelines under the **Chartered Accountants Act, 1949 (Code of Ethics 2020)**. Any local SEO or directory strategy must strictly follow these rules:

#### 1. Prohibition on Solicitation & Advertising (Clause 6 & 7, Part I, First Schedule)
- **Rule:** A member in practice cannot solicit clients or professional work, directly or indirectly, by circular, advertisement, personal communication, or interview.
- **Application to Directories:** Listing a firm in an online directory is **only permissible** if:
  - The directory organizes entries in alphabetical order or by category.
  - The firm does not pay for highlighted borders, special fonts, banner displays, or preferred "top-ranking" sponsored positions that set it apart from other members.
  - No claims of superiority (e.g., "#1 CA in Hyderabad", "Leading Tax Firm", "Best Auditors") are published.

#### 2. Permissible Details on Online Directories & Platforms
Under the Council Guidelines, a member/firm may provide only factual information:
- Firm Name and Firm Registration Number (FRN).
- Contact address, telephone numbers, and email address.
- Nature of professional services provided (descriptive, not promotional).
- Year of establishment (once officially verified) and partner qualifications.

#### 3. Website Guidelines (ICAI Notification dated 11th September 1998, revised 2020)
- **Pull Principle:** The firm's website must operate on a "pull" model, where information is accessed only by visitors who specifically seek it.
- **No Testimonials with Exaggerated Claims:** Displaying subjective client testimonials on the website is restricted unless permitted under specific institutional guidelines. Client logos must not be showcased without written consent.
- **No Fee Listings:** Fees or fee schedules must never be advertised publicly on the website or directory profiles.
